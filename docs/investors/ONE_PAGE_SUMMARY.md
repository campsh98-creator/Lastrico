# Motia — investor one-page summary

**Routes shaped around you.**  
**Product:** Lastrico by Motia — *Smoother roads. Smarter routes.*  
**Status:** investor working draft, 30 July 2026

## Opportunity

Navigation systems process sophisticated data, but users are usually offered a limited set of
controls. They cannot easily express combinations such as: “accept up to five extra minutes,
prefer major roads, and reduce known rough-surface exposure.”

Motia's thesis is that **the future interface of navigation is the prompt**. Natural language
would produce a bounded, versioned preference schema that the user confirms. Deterministic
routing—not an LLM—would enforce legal access, generate candidates, score evidence and
uncertainty, and explain the trade-off.

## Current product

Lastrico is an experimental open-source PWA tested in Milan. Repository evidence shows
MapLibre mapping, public Nominatim search, public Valhalla/OSRM routing, distinct car,
motorcycle, and bicycle modes, estimated rough-surface exposure, foreground GPS/manoeuvre
experiments, and road-report verification states.

It is not a production national navigator. Native mobile apps, contracted service levels,
nationwide hazards, traffic/weather, offline navigation, CarPlay, Android Auto, and
promptable routing are planned.

## Strategy

1. Stabilise and measure the Milan wedge.
2. Replace public demonstration services with a production regional backend.
3. Build reliable native phone navigation.
4. Expand through measured zones and a north–south corridor.
5. Validate bounded natural-language preferences.
6. Develop Motia Routing Intelligence and conditional B2B/API services.

Potential defensibility is the combined preference ontology, evidence quality, geospatial
pipelines, route scoring, feedback, moderation, integrations, community, and execution—not a
prompt or proprietary AI claim alone.

## Market entry and revenue hypotheses

Initial users are Milan motorcyclists/scooter riders, surface-sensitive drivers, cyclists,
and selected fleet or municipal design partners. Distribution starts through product
evidence, local search, founder-led demonstrations, specialist communities, open source,
earned media, referrals, and direct pilots.

Potential revenue includes B2C premium, paid/co-funded pilots, fleet subscriptions, and later
API or white-label intelligence. Pricing, retention, market size, and willingness to pay
remain validation items.

## Funding

The preferred Base model requires **€5.60m net financing across two events**: €1.50m modelled
in Q4 2026 and €4.10m in Q2 2028. Timing is conditional. This is not one proposed
crowdfunding campaign.

By Q4 2029, the Base model targets 400k MAU, 8% paid conversion, €10 quarterly net ARPPU, and
48 active B2B/B2G contracts at €8k quarterly recognised revenue. These are management targets,
not forecasts. Modelled closing cash is €2.017m, equal to 12.1 months at Q4 net burn.

Equity crowdfunding may be one financing component only after company, founder, valuation,
equity, authorised-platform, legal, tax, disclosure, anchor-demand, and campaign-cost review.
No campaign or return is guaranteed.

## Principal risks

Prototype maturity; route integrity; incomplete or stale evidence; provider dependence and
cost; privacy, security, moderation, and licensing; low retention or willingness to pay;
single-founder and hiring risk; automotive/store rejection; crowdfunding failure, dilution,
and follow-on funding.

Motia does not promise a safe route, accident prevention, nationwide hazard coverage,
platform approval, fundraising success, or investment return.
