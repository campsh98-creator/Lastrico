# Motia funding strategy

**Status:** management scenarios, not an offer or valuation  
**Planning date:** 30 July 2026  
**Preferred planning case:** Base, subject to quarterly reconciliation

## Funding principle

Motia should finance evidence in stages. Capital is released against product reliability,
data quality, moderation capacity, retention, unit economics, and commercial demand—not
against the size of the long-term vision alone.

Equity, debt, shareholder contributions, and crowdfunding proceeds are financing cash flows,
not operating revenue. Grants are shown separately even when recognised as operating income.

## Current capital context

Founder resources are described as extremely limited. The financial model therefore includes
sustainable founder compensation and complementary professional roles. It does not assume
that unpaid founder or community labour can indefinitely carry navigation, geospatial,
security, privacy, or moderation accountability.

## Financing sequence

### Readiness round

**Management model:** first net financing event of €0.70m–€2.20m in Q4 2026.

It may combine founder capital, angels, a regulated crowdfunding component, grants, or
strategic investment. The instrument, valuation, equity percentage, governance rights, and
tax treatment are deliberately unresolved.

Capital should fund:

- production-provider and route-integrity proof of concept;
- regional production backend;
- privacy, licence, insurance, and company work;
- complementary mobile/geospatial leadership;
- controlled testing and moderation;
- native phone alpha;
- measurable retention and route-quality evidence.

### Scale-validation round

**Management model:** second net financing event in Q1/Q2 2028 or Q4 2028 depending on
scenario.

It is conditional on:

- stable native phone navigation;
- audited coverage and moderation metrics;
- acceptable provider cost per session;
- repeat use and route acceptance;
- initial paid/co-funded pilots;
- a credible automotive entitlement and quality path;
- no unresolved material licence or privacy issue.

Capital should fund:

- two-platform mobile reliability;
- additional validated regions and corridors;
- Motia Routing Intelligence private beta;
- B2B integrations;
- automotive prototypes and testing;
- security, support, and data operations;
- repeatable commercial acquisition.

### Seed decision

A seed round is a decision gate, not a scheduled entitlement. It should be considered only
after Motia demonstrates repeatable demand, reliable routing, controlled data operations,
retention, and believable unit economics.

## Crowdfunding role

Equity crowdfunding may align with a community-supported product, but it is conditional.
Before any campaign:

1. Founder identity and biography are verified.
2. The issuer and legal structure are confirmed.
3. Counsel confirms the instrument, company-law, tax, and ECSPR treatment.
4. An authorised European crowdfunding service provider is selected from the ESMA register.
5. The platform accepts the issuer after due diligence.
6. A compliant key investment information sheet and risk presentation are prepared.
7. Campaign communications, financial model, cap table, and use of funds reconcile.
8. Anchor demand is tested without treating users or followers as investors.
9. Minimum funding still finances a coherent milestone set.
10. Post-campaign shareholder communication and governance are staffed.

In Italy, CONSOB states that since 11 November 2023 only business crowdfunding providers
authorised under Regulation (EU) 2020/1503 and listed in ESMA's register may operate.
Regulation (EU) 2020/1503 excludes offers above EUR 5 million aggregated over 12 months from
its scope. Specialist advice must determine the applicable route for Motia.

## Valuation and dilution

No precise valuation or equity percentage is approved. Current cumulative dilution
guardrails are **18–25% Lean, 22–32% Base, and 25–35% Accelerated**. These are cap-table
sensitivities, not market evidence, transaction terms, or an offer. They require founder,
investor, legal, and tax review.

Before presenting either:

- build a cap table;
- define the security and shareholder rights;
- compare pre-revenue mobility/software transactions cautiously;
- apply venture-method and milestone-based sensitivity analysis;
- model option-pool and follow-on dilution;
- explain prototype, single-founder, team, data, provider, and regulatory risk;
- test whether the proposed post-money valuation supports a later institutional round.

Any published equity range must be labelled indicative and founder/legal/advisor approved.

## End-of-model liquidity

Under the current assumptions:

- Lean closes Q4 2029 with approximately €657k and 12.0 months of runway measured at Q4 net
  burn.
- Base closes Q4 2029 with approximately €2.017m and 12.1 months of runway.
- Accelerated closes with approximately €4.316m and modelled positive quarterly operating
  cash flow in Q3 and Q4 2029; a conventional runway figure is therefore not meaningful.

These are scenario outputs, not forecasts. Positive cash flow in one model does not establish
durable break-even.

## Funding decision gates

| Gate | Evidence required | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign preparation | Verified company/founder data, authorised platform path, reconciled materials | Legal or platform eligibility unresolved |
| Public launch | Working demonstration, credible minimum use, anchor evidence, risk-complete page | Campaign depends on unsupported product or return claims |
| First close | Net proceeds finance a complete milestone set | Transaction costs or minimum target leave an unfunded plan |
| Second financing | Retention, reliability, coverage, pilots, unit cost | Growth increases burn without product evidence |
| Seed | Repeatable revenue/demand and governed data platform | Valuation depends mainly on unimplemented national/AI vision |
