# Motia market research required

**Purpose:** define the evidence still required before the Motia business plan, forecasts or
fundraising materials are presented as externally validated. This is a research backlog, not
a market-size claim.

## Priority evidence gaps

| Decision | Evidence required | Minimum method | Go/no-go signal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer problem | Frequency and severity of route-quality failures by car, motorcycle and bicycle users | 30–50 qualitative interviews across Milan, Rome, Catania and long-distance travellers; diary study | Repeated problem without prompting and identifiable switching behaviour |
| Route preferences | Which trade-offs users understand and trust | Prototype comparison tests with fastest route, explained alternative and unknown-data disclosure | Users correctly understand time, evidence and uncertainty |
| Willingness to pay | Price, plan and feature sensitivity | Van Westendorp plus live landing-page or in-product tests; never rely on stated intent alone | Retained paid conversion supports measured CAC/payback |
| Retention | Whether route intelligence creates recurring use | Cohort beta with D7/D30/D90 navigation retention | Retention stabilises before acquisition scale |
| B2B/B2G demand | Buyer, budget owner, procurement, ACV and implementation burden | 20+ discovery interviews; paid pilots; signed LOIs separated from contracts | At least three paid pilots with a repeatable buyer and measurable value |
| Competitors | Actual route controls, hazard sources, community flows, platform coverage and pricing | Current feature audit of Google Maps, Waze, Apple Maps, TomTom, HERE, Mapbox, Komoot and relevant Italian services | A defensible wedge that is not already a standard feature |
| Data supply | Coverage, licence, freshness, resolution and mode relevance | Source-by-source data inventory and sample imports for three different regions | Provenance-complete datasets improve routing beyond OSM alone |
| Community supply | Report completion, accuracy, abuse and moderation cost | Stopped-only reporting tests; blind verification sample; moderation time study | Acceptable verified-report yield and cost without unsafe interaction |
| Provider feasibility | Custom-route preservation, navigation terms, automotive support and unit price | Written technical proof of concept and commercial quote from at least two providers | Selected Motia route survives end-to-end with acceptable unit cost |
| National expansion | Cost and time to validate a new coverage zone | Milan, Rome and Catania zone pilots plus one north–south corridor | Repeatable zone release process with measured unknown coverage |
| Automotive | CarPlay and Android Auto eligibility and driver-distraction compliance | Native phone stability first; entitlement/submission preparation; simulator and vehicle tests | Platform-reviewed implementation; approval never assumed |
| Responsible impact | Whether suggestions change exposure without misleading users | Independent protocol comparing verified road-condition exposure and comprehension | Statistically and operationally defensible evidence before any impact claim |

## TAM, SAM and SOM work

Do not multiply all Italian trips, vehicles or smartphone users by an assumed subscription
price. Build a bottom-up market model:

1. Define mutually exclusive user segments and use occasions.
2. Establish the number of reachable users from official mobility and vehicle data.
3. Measure problem incidence, smartphone/app eligibility and expected active use.
4. Apply observed—not stated—paid conversion and retention.
5. Build B2B/B2G opportunity from named buyer counts, procurement cycles and validated ACV.
6. Keep API/platform licensing as optional until integration demand exists.
7. Report TAM, SAM and three-year SOM separately, with sensitivity ranges and no double
   counting between consumer, fleet and institutional users.

## Research governance

- Store interview consent, scripts, sample, exclusions and raw results.
- Separate founder hypotheses from respondent evidence.
- Predefine success thresholds before experiments.
- Record source URL, publisher, access date, licence, reference period and methodology.
- Do not copy hazard or map data from proprietary consumer maps.
- Minimise location data and ask counsel whether the final processing requires a DPIA.
- Publish negative findings and unresolved uncertainty in investor materials.
- Reconcile every accepted market assumption to
  `financials/MOTIA_FINANCIAL_ASSUMPTIONS.md`; otherwise leave it out of the forecast.
