# Motia business plan Draft 1 — critical review

**Review stance:** sceptical investor, CFO, product, technical, legal/privacy, and community
review. This is intentionally adversarial and does not approve fundraising or publication.

## Overall conclusion

Draft 1 presents an ambitious but rationally staged thesis. The repository proves that the
founder can frame and prototype one narrow surface-routing problem. It does not yet prove
that users return, that institutions pay, that Italian hazard data is obtainable at useful
quality, or that a provider can preserve a Motia-selected route in native navigation.

The plan is investable only as a validation programme. Draft 2 must reduce narrative
overreach, make the economic model auditable, define explicit gates, and expose the largest
unknowns rather than burying them in disclaimers.

## Findings

| ID | Severity | Finding | Required correction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CR-01 | Critical | No measured traction, user cohort, or paid-pilot evidence exists. | Put current evidence and absent evidence in one investor-readable table; make 2026–27 a validation thesis. |
| CR-02 | Critical | The funding ask is discussed before a reconciled quarterly cash model. | Derive the preferred raise from the 13-quarter model, show cash trough, runway, contingency, and next-round dependence. |
| CR-03 | Critical | Equity crowdfunding could be mistaken for the default or an imminent launch. | Make it one conditional financing path; list readiness gates, failure consequences, dilution discipline, and alternatives. |
| CR-04 | High | The addressable market is asserted qualitatively without a defendable SAM/SOM. | Remove unsupported market size and add bottom-up research requirements based on segments, corridors, fleets, and procurement. |
| CR-05 | High | The moat is weakly defined against incumbents. | Define defensibility as verified, versioned condition evidence; preference schema; route-ranking IP; integrations; trust; and partner workflow—not a prompt UI alone. |
| CR-06 | High | “AI routing” may be read as non-deterministic or safety-critical. | Add a formal responsibility boundary: LLM intent parsing; deterministic legality, candidates, scoring, rerouting, and audit. |
| CR-07 | High | Native/automotive scope may exceed a pre-seed team's capacity. | Sequence one primary native platform, preserve optionality, and gate second platform/automotive work on field and entitlement evidence. |
| CR-08 | High | Official and community data are described as strategic without proven access or operating moderation. | Create source-by-source due diligence and data-quality gates; state that current report states do not prove moderation operations. |
| CR-09 | High | Safety relevance may be conflated with a safety outcome. | Repeat the claim hierarchy and prohibit crash-reduction claims until independently supported. |
| CR-10 | High | Founder profile creates key-person and technical-depth risk. | Add verification banner, complementary hiring map, advisory needs, governance, and milestone ownership beyond the founder. |
| CR-11 | Medium | The customer sequence lacks ICP-specific pains, buyer, budget, and sales cycle. | Add hypothesis cards for consumers, fleets, and municipalities with explicit discovery tests. |
| CR-12 | Medium | Open source is treated as inherently positive without an economic boundary. | Define what remains open, what can be proprietary/hosted, contributor licence/data provenance, moderation burden, and success metrics. |
| CR-13 | Medium | Pricing hypotheses are not tied to unit costs or contribution margin. | Define B2C net ARPU, store effects, navigation cost/session, B2B ACV, gross margin, CAC, payback, and sensitivity. |
| CR-14 | Medium | Roadmap milestones lack quantitative go/no-go thresholds. | Add retention, route acceptance, latency, data freshness, moderation, pilot and unit-cost thresholds. |
| CR-15 | Medium | Legal/privacy work appears as a checklist, not a product constraint. | Add privacy-by-design gates, explicit location minimisation, UGC operations, licensing register, and Article 35 assessment. |
| CR-16 | Medium | “Italy-wide” can sound like immediate national coverage. | Reframe as corridor-by-corridor release with coverage/unknown metrics and no national claim before evidence. |
| CR-17 | Medium | Founder-led marketing may become a dependency and expertise-performance risk. | Add editorial cadence, evidence rules, backup voices, owned audience, and a sustainability limit. |
| CR-18 | Low | Coined-name pronunciation and brand protection are unresolved. | Mark every identity as provisional and include trademark/domain/founder approval gates. |

## Investor questions Draft 2 must answer

1. What does a €1 of funding validate, and by when?
2. What measurable user behaviour proves the route is better for that user?
3. How much unknown condition data can a route contain before Motia declines to recommend
   an alternative?
4. Who pays first, who signs, and how long does a sale take?
5. Can the selected route be navigated faithfully with the chosen provider?
6. What is the cost per active user and navigation session at 10k, 100k, and 250k MAU?
7. What remains open source, and what produces durable paid value?
8. What happens if crowdfunding fails or is delayed by six months?
9. Which milestones require a senior geospatial/routing hire?
10. What evidence would cause Motia to stop, narrow, or change direction?

## Financial review requirements

Draft 2 must:

- reconcile Lean, Base, and Accelerated scenario revenue, operating cost, financing, and
  closing cash across Q4 2026–Q4 2029;
- show founder compensation explicitly;
- separate grants from recurring revenue and financing from all operating income;
- state campaign/legal/transaction cost and net proceeds;
- connect marketing budget to the operating model;
- label valuation and equity ranges illustrative and methodology-dependent;
- calculate runway with a transparent method;
- avoid summing alternative pre-seed and seed amounts into one misleading ask.

## Release recommendation

Do not externally circulate Draft 1 as an investor document. Proceed to Draft 2 only after
the financial source files exist. The final document may be circulated as an **Investor
Working Draft** after founder biography, entity, cap table, funding path, and legal wording
are separately confirmed.
