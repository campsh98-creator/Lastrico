**Status:** conditional 120-second campaign draft  
**Founder verification, legal review, platform approval, and production edit required**

Do not publish this as an investment solicitation outside an authorised and approved

## Production direction

- Founder on camera; no generated or substitute portrait.
- Use real beta screen recordings and clearly label them “Current Milan beta”.
- Label future diagrams “Planned” or “Long-term vision”.
- No simulated user counts, testimonials, platform approvals, or national coverage.
- Captions required; calm pace; no urgency countdown or return language.

## Script

**[0:00–0:15 — Founder on camera]**

Hello, I am Domenico Campanella Scali, founder of Motia.

Have you ever followed a navigation shortcut that saved two minutes on paper, but sent you
onto a road that was rough, narrow, or simply unsuitable for the journey you wanted?

**[On-screen: “Founder verification required before external publication.”]**

**[0:15–0:35 — Real beta comparison]**

I started with a problem I could make concrete in Milan: cobblestones. A motorcyclist,
cyclist, or driver may willingly accept a small delay for a smoother known alternative.

Lastrico by Motia is our current experimental open-source PWA. It compares route candidates
using known road-surface evidence for cars, motorcycles, and bicycles.

**[On-screen: “Current beta. Milan test coverage. Public beta providers. No production SLA.”]**

**[0:35–0:55 — Limitations]**

This is an early product, not a finished national navigator. The evidence can be incomplete
or outdated. We do not promise a safe route or accident prevention. Native apps, nationwide
hazards, CarPlay, Android Auto, and conversational routing are future milestones.

**[0:55–1:20 — Motia vision]**

The larger idea is Motia: *Routes shaped around you.*

Our thesis is that the future interface of navigation is the prompt. You might say:

“I can accept five extra minutes. Prefer major roads and reduce known rough-surface exposure.”

The prompt would not invent a route. It would become a bounded preference profile that you
review. Deterministic routing would still enforce laws, validate roads, calculate
alternatives, and explain uncertainty.

**[1:20–1:40 — Community and capital]**

The community can help test routes, correct evidence, improve code, and contribute local
knowledge. But high-impact data remains moderated, versioned, and accountable.

Capital would fund production infrastructure, native mobile engineering, geospatial data,
field testing, privacy, security, moderation, and selected regional expansion.

**[1:40–1:55 — Conditional investment language]**

Investing in an early-stage company is risky and illiquid. Product, adoption, fundraising,
platform approval, and returns are not guaranteed. The complete risks, financial
assumptions, equity terms, and use of funds must be reviewed on the authorised campaign
page.

**[1:55–2:00 — Close]**

If the evidence convinces you, join us as a tester, contributor, partner—or, when a regulated
campaign is ready, consider the investment on its merits.

Motia. *Routes shaped around you.*
