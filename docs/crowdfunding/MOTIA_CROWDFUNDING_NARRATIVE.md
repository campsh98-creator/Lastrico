# Motia crowdfunding narrative

**Status:** conditional campaign working draft—not approved for publication  
**Not an offer, prospectus, key investment information sheet, or investment advice**

## Routes shaped around you

Navigation can calculate an impressive amount of information, yet people still struggle to
express the journey they actually want.

A route that appears a few minutes faster may cross cobblestones that a motorcyclist would
rather avoid, send a driver onto a narrow rural shortcut, or use a damaged surface that does
not suit the vehicle or passenger. Existing products are sophisticated, but the controls
visible to users are often too rigid to express these trade-offs.

Motia is being built around one thesis:

> **The future interface of navigation is the prompt.**

That does not mean an AI chatbot should invent roads or replace routing algorithms. It means
people should be able to explain priorities in plain language, confirm how those priorities
were interpreted, and compare legal, evidence-based alternatives.

## The first working step: Lastrico by Motia

Lastrico is an experimental open-source PWA currently tested in Milan. It compares routes
for cars, motorcycles, and bicycles using known road-surface evidence, with a focus on rough
and irregular surfaces.

The current beta demonstrates:

- route calculation through public beta providers;
- distinct car, motorcycle, and bicycle modes;
- estimated road-surface exposure;
- route alternatives and trade-offs;
- foreground GPS and manoeuvre experiments;
- community report submission states;
- a public open-source repository.

It is not yet a production national navigator. Native mobile applications, production
service levels, nationwide hazards, traffic, weather, offline navigation, CarPlay, Android
Auto, and conversational routing are planned—not working.

## Why begin with road surfaces?

Milan cobblestones create a specific and understandable test. A rider or driver may accept
four or five additional minutes for a smoother known alternative. The product must show the
delay, evidence, and unknown coverage rather than declare one route “safe”.

If Motia can reliably model this trade-off, it can test a broader road-intelligence approach:

- damaged roads and potholes where evidence is recent;
- roadworks, closures, flooding, or debris through authorised sources;
- continuity on suitable major roads for long journeys;
- vehicle-specific preferences;
- conditional toll avoidance;
- simpler route profiles;
- user-defined combinations of time, comfort, cost, and uncertainty.

Each new factor requires source, licence, freshness, confidence, mode relevance, and
validation. Missing evidence never means hazard-free.

## A community-supported product

Motia intends to combine accountable professional engineering with community participation.
Developers can improve code, documentation, accessibility, and tests. Road users can submit
plain-language observations while stopped or after a trip.

Community evidence does not change routes immediately. Reports need provenance, confidence,
expiry, corroboration, moderation, abuse controls, and an appeal path. Personal location
history and licensed or private datasets are not made public merely because the application
is open source.

A future financing round would fund evidence, not a finished national claim:

- production map, geocoding, routing, and data services;

The preferred Base financial scenario currently models **€5.60 million net financing across
two separate events through 2029**. This is the full scenario requirement, not a proposed
single crowdfunding campaign. The first Base event is modelled at €1.50 million net in Q4

Crowdfunding could be one part of a financing mix with angels, strategic investors, grants,
or later institutional funding. Under the current European framework, a single campaign
cannot simply be assumed to finance the Base plan.

## Conditional campaign milestones

A campaign minimum must finance a coherent milestone set. Candidate funded milestones are:

1. Replace public demonstration services as critical production dependencies.
2. Establish the production regional backend and data governance.
3. Recruit complementary mobile/geospatial leadership.
4. Release a controlled native phone beta on the selected initial platform.
5. Validate route completion, retention, data quality, and moderation capacity.
6. Complete at least one paid or co-funded partner pilot.

Stretch work—second mobile platform, more regions, automotive prototypes, and Motia Routing
Intelligence—must remain conditional on net funds and evidence.

## The investment case

Potential reasons to study Motia include:

- a concrete working wedge rather than an abstract AI concept;
- a broader preference and road-intelligence platform thesis;
- a community and open-source development mechanism;
- consumer, fleet, municipal, and API hypotheses;
- a staged validation plan;
- transparent acknowledgement of technical, data, legal, and market risk.

These are hypotheses, not proof of future returns.

## Material risks

Investors could lose some or all invested capital. Material risks include:

- prototype-stage product and single-founder dependency;
- incomplete or inaccurate road evidence;
- difficulty achieving reliable navigation;
- dependence on external map and routing providers;
- provider costs and licensing limits;
- privacy, security, moderation, and liability exposure;
- CarPlay, Android Auto, and app-store rejection;
- low user retention or willingness to pay;
- dominant competitors;
- inability to recruit key expertise;
- unsuccessful follow-on financing;
- dilution and an illiquid investment;
- campaign failure or delay;
- trademark or company-name conflict.

No route-safety, fundraising, platform-approval, valuation, revenue, or return outcome is
guaranteed.

## Regulatory condition

In Italy, business crowdfunding providers must be authorised under Regulation (EU)
2020/1503 and listed in ESMA's register. The regulation excludes offers above EUR 5 million
aggregated over 12 months from its scope. A campaign requires an authorised platform,
professional legal/accounting/tax review, due diligence, a key investment information sheet,
compliant marketing, and post-investment governance.
