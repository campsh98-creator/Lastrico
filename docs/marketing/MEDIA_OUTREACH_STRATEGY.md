# Media and creator outreach strategy

## Editorial proposition

Earn attention by offering an inspectable story:

- a founder turning a specific Milan road-surface problem into an open-source experiment;
- the gap between fastest-time optimisation and explicit route preferences;
- the difficulty of responsible community road information;
- what a future conversational preference interface can and cannot do.

Lastrico is the current evidence. Motia is the broader thesis. Neither national coverage,
commercial traction, AI performance, funding, nor automotive-platform approval may be
implied.

## Target categories

1. Italian technology and startup journalists.
2. Mobility, automotive, motorcycle, and cycling creators.
3. Open-source, open-data, and civic-tech communities.
4. Milan local media and university outlets.
5. Product, design, and geospatial podcasts/newsletters.
6. Fundraising and founder-journey creators after the data room is credible.

Named people, including Marcello Ascani, are aspirational research examples only. Record no
relationship, interest, or endorsement until it exists.

## Credibility prerequisites

- public beta or recorded demo that works for the stated scenario;
- coverage and current/planned labels;
- reproducible test with source and date;
- current screenshots and short demo;
- founder biography marked **Founder verification required before external publication**;
- concise safety, privacy, provider, and data limitations;
- repository, licence, contribution guide, and correction route;
- approved factsheet, logo concept status, and contact owner;
- no confidential or personal data.

## Outreach sequence

1. Research the target's recent work and audience.
2. Select one editorial angle and one relevant proof asset.
3. Send a personal message under 150 words.
4. Follow up once after 5–7 working days with new evidence, not “just checking”.
5. Close the loop after a second unanswered message.
6. Record consent, response, coverage, correction needs, and outcome in the target template.

Never automate mass outreach or purchase contact lists.

## Pitch template

**Subject:** Evidence for a story on [specific route-choice problem]

Hi [name],

Your [specific article/video] examined [relevant issue]. I am building Lastrico by Motia, an
open-source Milan beta that currently compares routes using known road-surface information
for cars, motorcycles, and bicycles. It is an experiment, not a safety-certified navigator
or a nationally available service.

We have documented [one reproducible test/correction] and can share the method, limitations,
demo, and source. The broader editorial question is [one angle], not a request for a generic
promotion.

Would a [10-minute demo / evidence pack / founder interview] be useful? A factual brief is
here: [link].

Domenico Campanella Scali  
Founder, Motia — **biography verification required before publication**

## Founder talking points

- the concrete route problem that initiated Lastrico;
- what the Milan beta demonstrably does today;
- why unknown road data remains unknown;
- why natural language is planned as a preference interface, not the routing engine;
- why open source improves inspection but does not automatically produce adoption;
- missing complementary expertise and the next validation milestone.

## Media kit

Include a one-page factsheet, current/planned matrix, product screenshots, provisional brand
assets labelled as such, founder bio pending verification, safe demo recording, repository
and beta links, licences/attribution, contact details, and a correction policy. Add a founder
photograph only after identity, consent, and publication approval.

## Outcome measures

Track personalised pitches, replies, qualified conversations, demo requests, earned pieces,
message accuracy, referral visits, beta activations, community joins, and correction rate.
Coverage volume without qualified action or accurate framing is not success.
