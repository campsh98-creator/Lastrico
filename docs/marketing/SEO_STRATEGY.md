# Motia and Lastrico SEO strategy

**Status:** planned, low-cost acquisition programme. No ranking or traffic claims are made.

## Search architecture

Use **Motia** for the parent thesis and future platform. Use **Lastrico by Motia** for pages
that describe the current Milan beta and its verified road-surface use case.

| Cluster | Search intent | Example Italian queries | First useful page |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Milan road surfaces | solve a local trip problem | evitare sanpietrini Milano; percorso senza pavé | tested Milan guide with current coverage |
| Motorcycle and bicycle comfort | compare route suitability | percorso moto senza pavé; navigatore per moto Milano | mode-specific route test |
| Damaged roads | understand/report a condition | evitare strade dissestate; app per evitare buche | reporting and data-confidence explainer |
| Personalised navigation | learn about alternatives to time-only routing | navigatore personalizzato; percorso più comodo | multi-objective routing explainer |
| Prompted navigation | understand the future concept | navigatore con preferenze personalizzate | Motia product-vision page |
| Open-source routing | contribute or evaluate code | open source route planner; OSM routing project | developer and contribution guide |
| Future local research | signal interest outside coverage | strade dissestate Catania | research page, never a fake product landing page |

Queries are starting hypotheses, not verified search-volume estimates.

## Priority page sequence

1. Product page: what Lastrico currently does, Milan coverage, modes, providers, limitations.
2. Evidence page: one reproducible fastest-versus-lower-exposure route test.
3. Contribution page: code, testing, road-data provenance, and non-technical participation.
4. Explainer: why time alone is an incomplete route preference.
5. Founder/build log with dated product evidence.
6. FAQ covering safety, privacy, data freshness, supported areas, and current/planned
   functionality.
7. City pages only after verified local content and a named update owner exist.

Do not create templated pages that imply Catania, Rome, or nationwide product coverage.
Research-interest pages must say that routing support is not available there today.

## Technical checklist

- one canonical URL per page and stable English/Italian locale paths when localisation ships;
- unique titles and descriptions that identify current beta status;
- indexable server-rendered text, semantic headings, descriptive image alternatives;
- XML sitemap, robots rules, correct redirects, HTTPS, mobile performance, and clean 404s;
- Product/SoftwareApplication structured data only for demonstrable product facts;
- FAQ schema only when the visible page contains the same answers;
- Organisation schema only after legal company details are confirmed;
- breadcrumb and Article schema for editorial content where applicable;
- Open Graph assets without city-locked branding;
- Core Web Vitals, broken-link, accessibility, and structured-data checks;
- no personal coordinates, location histories, or unmoderated reports in indexed pages.

## Content quality template

Every route or city article must include:

- date, author/reviewer, tested area, modes, and method;
- map/data providers and attribution;
- known versus unknown data;
- visual evidence recorded safely;
- current product availability and limitations;
- correction/report link;
- review or expiry date.

Internal links should connect problem pages to evidence, evidence to the beta, beta to
limitations, and contribution pages to governance.

## Low-cost tools

Start with first-party product analytics, Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools,
PageSpeed Insights, schema validators, a privacy-reviewed web analytics tool, GitHub link
checks, and a simple keyword/landing-page spreadsheet. Paid SEO suites require a specific
research need and budget approval.

## Authority and backlinks

Earn links through reproducible route studies, open-source releases, university or civic-tech
collaboration, conference notes, data-method documentation, and useful local research. Never
buy links, trade undisclosed endorsements, or manufacture city pages.

## Measurement

Track non-branded and branded clicks, qualified landing sessions, search-to-beta registration,
search-to-first-route activation, return rate, backlinks from relevant domains, corrections,
and page freshness. Use Search Console data after enough impressions exist; do not invent
traffic forecasts.

## Risks

- **Premature national pages:** prevent with a coverage ledger and editorial approval.
- **“AI navigator” overclaim:** reserve AI terms for transparent preference translation that
  actually exists; describe it as planned until implemented.
- **Stale road claims:** display observation and review dates.
- **Thin city pages:** publish only when evidence is locally useful.
- **Search dependence:** convert useful visits to permission-based email and community.
- **Language duplication:** use canonical/hreflang rules after professional review.
