# Motia marketing strategy

**Status:** internal working plan, July 2026  
**Principle:** build in public, solve a visible problem, let the community improve the
product, and convert trust into adoption.

## Product truth used in every campaign

- **Current fact:** Lastrico by Motia is an experimental open-source PWA whose tested beta
  area is Milan. It compares car, motorcycle, and bicycle routes using known road-surface
  information.
- **Planned:** Motia Routing Intelligence may expand the number of route signals, regions,
  interfaces, and commercial services only after product, data, legal, and reliability
  gates are passed.
- **Long-term vision:** natural language may translate user intent into bounded, reviewable
  routing preferences. It will not replace deterministic routing, legal restrictions, or
  safety-critical controls.

No communication may claim a safe route, accident prevention, national coverage, real-time
hazard detection, artificial-intelligence performance, commercial traction, platform
approval, or partnerships without evidence.

## Objectives through 2027

1. Validate that a recognisable road-quality problem brings qualified people to the beta.
2. Learn whether those people complete a first route and return.
3. Build a small, useful contributor community around code, testing, documentation, and
   responsibly sourced road information.
4. Establish an owned audience through permission-based email.
5. Earn credible coverage through evidence, not launch theatre.
6. Identify one or two acquisition loops worth funding; keep broad paid acquisition gated.

Targets are set only after a four-week baseline. Until then, every numerical threshold in
this plan is a management hypothesis.

## Positioning and messages

- **Motia:** routing intelligence designed around explicit user preferences.
- **Lastrico by Motia:** the first focused experiment, starting with known road surfaces.
- **Founder voice:** curious, practical, transparent, and willing to show failures. Domenico
  Campanella Scali may discuss the founder thesis and learning process, but must not present
  himself as a senior AI researcher, routing architect, geospatial scientist, or established
  mobility executive.
- **Proof hierarchy:** working demo, reproducible route test, source and limitation,
  contributor evidence, then vision.

## Organic distribution system

One evidence-rich weekly asset drives the system:

1. Record a safe route test, build update, or technical explanation.
2. Publish one long-form source: YouTube video, article, or development note.
3. Derive short video, carousel, LinkedIn post, GitHub update, newsletter note, and community
   question.
4. Link every asset to one measurable next action.
5. Review product-linked outcomes after seven and thirty days.

Primary channels are GitHub, search, YouTube, LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, selected local
groups, university networks, and earned media. An email list is the owned channel. GitHub
Discussions is the recommended first community home; do not launch several empty chat
servers.

## Paid-media gates

Paid distribution is allowed only when:

- the beta and landing page are available to the intended audience;
- activation, route success, seven-day retention, and referral events are measurable;
- organic content identifies a repeatable audience-message combination;
- a maximum test budget, duration, and stop condition are approved;
- the experiment does not incentivise low-quality road reports.

Funding an unvalidated channel, buying followers, buying email lists, or describing financing
as customer demand is out of scope.

## Main risks and controls

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Early signal | Control | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Community remains inactive | medium | medium | no first contribution in 8 weeks | smaller guided tasks and direct onboarding | founder/community lead |
| Content does not activate users | high | high | views rise while first routes do not | optimise product CTA and audience fit | founder/product |
| Inaccurate road claim harms trust | medium | high | correction or dispute | provenance, review, visible uncertainty | data owner |
| Founder production is unsustainable | high | medium | missed cadence for 3 weeks | batch production and reduce channels | founder |
| Moderation overwhelms the team | medium | high | response backlog exceeds 7 days | intake limits and trusted reviewers | community owner |
| Paid acquisition is uneconomic | medium | high | cost per activated user breaches cap | stop test; return to evidence loop | growth owner |
| Consumer messaging weakens B2B credibility | medium | medium | partner confusion | separate pages and case evidence | founder |
| Platform algorithm change reduces reach | medium | medium | >30% reach decline | email, search, GitHub diversification | growth owner |

## Dependencies and review

The quarterly budget must reconcile with the company financial model before external use.
Founder biography, company legal form, trademarks, privacy roles, pricing, fundraising,
partnerships, and market claims require confirmation. Review this strategy monthly during
the beta and quarterly after a stable baseline exists.
