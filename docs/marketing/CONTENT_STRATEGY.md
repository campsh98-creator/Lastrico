# Founder-led content strategy

## Editorial promise

Show the work, the evidence, the limitations, and what changed. Content exists to help
people understand a real routing problem and decide whether to test or improve Lastrico—not
to make Motia appear more mature than it is.

## Voice

Domenico Campanella Scali is the visible founder: practical, curious, ambitious, open to
correction, and technically informed without claiming unverified expertise. Use “we are
testing”, “the current beta”, “management hypothesis”, and “planned” precisely.

## Content pillars

1. Product development in public.
2. Real route-choice problems and controlled comparisons.
3. Road-information provenance, confidence, and uncertainty.
4. Routing and preference concepts explained simply.
5. Open-source contributions and corrections.
6. Founder decisions, mistakes, costs, and learning.
7. Urban mobility, motorcycles, bicycles, and cars without mode tokenism.
8. The Motia thesis: explicit preferences beyond a time-only objective.

## Weekly minimum viable cadence

| Asset | Cadence | Owner | Primary CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| evidence-rich source asset | weekly | founder | test, comment, or join |
| founder build note | weekly | founder | follow the roadmap |
| short video | twice weekly | founder/editor | view evidence or beta |
| GitHub/community update | weekly | maintainer | scoped contribution |
| segmented email | twice monthly | founder | one audience-specific action |
| contributor spotlight | monthly when earned | community owner | recognise useful work |

Reduce frequency before reducing factual review. No spotlight is published without consent.

## Evidence-first formats

- route test with method, modes, providers, date, and unknown data;
- “bug, cause, correction, verification” development diary;
- before/after community correction after moderation;
- one routing concept in plain language;
- founder decision memo showing trade-offs;
- roadmap status: shipped, testing, blocked, or future;
- monthly metrics note that includes failures and denominator context.

Driving footage must be captured by a passenger, fixed equipment configured before travel,
controlled testing, or post-journey/simulated playback. Never ask a driver or rider to
interact with content while moving.

## Repurposing workflow

1. Select one question from user research, product telemetry, or a documented test.
2. Write the evidence card: claim, source, date, limitation, current/planned label.
3. Record one 6–12 minute source asset.
4. Produce up to four clips, one article, one LinkedIn post, one community question, and one
   email segment.
5. Add captions, attribution, correction route, consent check, and CTA.
6. Publish over seven days.
7. Compare qualified actions—not only views—after 7 and 30 days.
8. Archive raw personal location material under an approved retention policy or delete it.

## Editorial review

Before publication verify:

- product state and geographic coverage;
- safe filming and privacy redaction;
- road-data provenance and uncertainty;
- no “safe route”, accident-prevention, national readiness, or unverified AI claim;
- no fabricated user count, partnership, endorsement, qualification, or founder expertise;
- one clear audience and CTA;
- accessible captions, transcript, colour contrast, and image alternatives;
- correction owner and review date.

## Sustainable founder production

Batch one half-day per week, maintain a four-item backlog, use reusable layouts, and cap
active channels. If the founder misses three consecutive weeks, reduce the cadence and
delegate editing before adding a new channel. Founder visibility is an asset, not the sole
brand or an unpaid substitute for specialist product, safety, data, legal, or engineering
roles.
