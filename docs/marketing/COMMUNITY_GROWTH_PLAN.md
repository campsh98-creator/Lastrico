# Community growth plan

## Recommendation

Use:

- **GitHub Issues and pull requests** for accountable technical and data work;
- **GitHub Discussions** as the first shared community space for beta feedback, public
  questions, roadmap discussion, and founder updates;
- **segmented email** for consented announcements.

Do not launch Discord, Telegram, Slack, Reddit, and a forum simultaneously. Reassess a
consumer chat channel only after Discussions has at least one active moderator, weekly
activity for eight weeks, and a clear unmet synchronous need.

## Contributor and user funnel

| Stage | Desired experience | Measure | Support |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Discover | sees a concrete problem and evidence | qualified visit | route test or build note |
| Understand | knows current capability and limits | documentation completion | current/planned summary |
| Join | chooses technical, tester, local, or research path | consented join | labelled Discussion category |
| First contribution | completes a bounded useful action | first valid contribution | guided issue/checklist |
| Recognition | receives specific acknowledgement | time to acknowledgement | release note/profile credit |
| Repeat | contributes again within 90 days | repeat-contributor rate | follow-up task and feedback |
| Trusted role | accepts scoped responsibility | active trusted contributors | documented maintainer decision |

## Community categories

- Announcements and weekly founder update.
- Beta help and reproducible feedback.
- Route-test methods and results.
- Road-data provenance and corrections.
- Development and beginner contributions.
- Product ideas and roadmap questions.

Emergency reports and immediate road-danger response are out of scope; direct users to the
appropriate public authority without inventing emergency guidance.

## Non-technical participation

Offer a guided form that asks for a public road segment, municipality, travel mode,
observation date, condition category, source/permission, and non-sensitive evidence. Explain
that a report is pending, may be rejected, and does not change routing until reviewed. Never
ask for a home address, continuous journey, identifiable person, licence plate, or unsafe
on-road interaction.

Useful first actions include reproducing a route, checking instructions at a safe stop,
improving plain-language documentation, translating approved copy, testing accessibility,
or validating a public source.

## Recognition

Use release-note acknowledgements, contributor lists, opt-in profiles, community badges,
testing-group invitations, and periodic contributor spotlights. Recognition follows
verified usefulness, not volume. Do not promise employment, equity, premium access, or an
advisory role without a separate written decision.

## Moderation and ownership

The founder/release owner remains accountable for product claims and releases. Assign named
owners for security, routing, road data, moderation, and community before scale. Trusted
contributors may review within documented limits; critical safety, privacy, security,
routing, and data-governance work cannot depend indefinitely on unpaid labour.

Target a first response within five working days during the beta, explicitly as a
best-effort target. Pause intake when the oldest actionable report is over 14 days or when
moderation quality cannot be maintained.

## Referral loop

After a useful beta action, invite one of three choices: share a non-location-specific
product page, invite a relevant tester, or improve a documented public-road issue. Never
reward unverified report volume, spam, fake accounts, or unsafe evidence collection.

## Milestones

- **0–25 engaged members:** founder replies, five guided issues, one weekly update.
- **25–100:** first repeat contributors, monthly call if demanded, moderation playbook.
- **100–300:** trusted-review pilot, category leads, quarterly community health report.
- **300+:** decide whether a dedicated consumer channel and paid community support are
  justified.

These are management thresholds, not growth forecasts.
