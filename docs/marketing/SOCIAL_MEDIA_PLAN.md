# Social media plan

## Channel roles

| Channel | Role | Audience | Cadence | Do not use it for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | founder decisions, milestones, technical/business learning | contributors, partners, investors | 1–2 posts/week | inflated traction |
| Instagram | visual route evidence, carousels, community stories | beta users and local mobility groups | 2 posts/week | generic motivation |
| TikTok | concise problem/evidence/explanation videos | consumer discovery | 2 tests/week | unsafe filming or trend chasing |
| GitHub | source, decisions, issues, release evidence | technical contributors | weekly update | consumer support only |
| Reddit/forums/groups | answer relevant existing discussions | local and mode communities | selective | link dropping or mass posting |
| Email | owned, segmented progress | consented audiences | twice monthly | mixing investors, media, and users |

Do not open additional channels until the current cadence has an owner and produces
product-linked learning for eight weeks.

## Short-form template

1. **0–2 seconds:** concrete problem—“This route saves four minutes, but what did it trade
   away?”
2. **Evidence:** map comparison, controlled footage, or a documented screen recording.
3. **Insight:** one current product fact or routing concept.
4. **Limitation:** tested area, unknown data, or planned status.
5. **CTA:** test the Milan beta, inspect the code, report a public road segment, or join a
   research conversation.

Examples include fastest versus lower-known-rough-surface exposure, a fixed-camera
motorcycle perspective, one community correction, and a user preference translated into a
mock schema explicitly labelled future concept.

## Community conduct

- Ask permission before featuring a contributor or their content.
- Never repost personal coordinates, licence plates, faces, home addresses, or identifiable
  travel histories.
- Correct road claims publicly and link to the correction.
- Do not reward report quantity; recognise verified usefulness.
- Respond to route-error reports with a reporting path, not a defensive safety claim.
- Declare sponsored, affiliate, creator, or investor relationships.

## Experiment rhythm

For each post record audience, hypothesis, production time, distribution cost, reach,
qualified visits, beta registrations, first routes, community joins, and valid
contributions. Review after 7 and 30 days. Stop formats that attract views without useful
actions after three comparable tests.

## Q4 2026 series

- **Route choice of the week:** one controlled comparison.
- **What failed this week:** transparent product correction.
- **One minute of routing:** accessible technical explanation.
- **Contributor path:** one beginner task or useful correction.
- **Founder ledger:** time, cost, learning, and next test.

All schedules remain conditional on product readiness and safe production capacity.
