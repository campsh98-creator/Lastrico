# Marketing experiment system

## Rules

Each experiment has one audience, hypothesis, primary outcome, maximum duration, approved
budget, privacy/safety review, and stop condition. Product reliability and region support
gate acquisition. Followers, impressions, and email addresses are not substitutes for
activation or retention.

Use `marketing/GROWTH_EXPERIMENT_BACKLOG.csv` as the execution register.

## Prioritisation

Score each proposal from 1–5 on:

- evidence strength;
- expected learning value;
- audience and product readiness;
- reversibility;
- cost and founder time;
- privacy, safety, moderation, and claim risk.

Prioritise high-learning, low-risk organic tests. Paid amplification requires an organic
signal and an activated-user cost ceiling.

## Initial portfolio

1. **Milan motorcycle beta cohort:** test whether a bounded local community produces
   repeatable route-quality feedback.
2. **Milan pavé evidence page:** test problem-intent search traffic and first-route
   activation.
3. **Founder origin video:** test whether transparent problem framing drives qualified beta
   visits.
4. **Beginner open-source issue week:** test contributor onboarding, not star count.
5. **Route-comparison short:** compare qualified action per production hour.
6. **University clinic:** test whether a supervised small event produces usable bugs or
   data-method improvements.
7. **Five personalised local-media pitches:** test the editorial evidence package.
8. **Creator evidence pitch:** test one aspirational creator category without assuming
   access.
9. **Future-region research waitlist:** test research interest while stating no current
   coverage.
10. **Community road-mapping session:** proceed only with moderation capacity and safe
    evidence rules.

## Experiment readout

Record:

- setup and exact claim;
- audience and sample limitations;
- spend and founder hours;
- reach, qualified visit, registration, activation, retention, and valid contribution;
- adverse outcomes and correction requests;
- decision: stop, revise, repeat, or scale;
- what would falsify the interpretation.

Do not generalise from one city cohort, a small creator response, or self-selected community
participants to the national market.

## Quarterly decision

At quarter end:

1. close expired tests;
2. compare channel cohorts through activation and retention;
3. remove experiments that cannot be measured safely;
4. fund at most the one or two strongest loops;
5. publish an honest internal learning note;
6. reconcile next-quarter approved spend with the financial model.
