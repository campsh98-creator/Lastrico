# Contributor onboarding

Welcome to Lastrico by Motia. The current project is an experimental open-source Milan PWA
that compares route alternatives for cars, motorcycles, and bicycles using known
road-surface information. Motia's broader routing-intelligence platform is planned, not
implemented.

## Choose a path

- **Code:** fix a labelled issue, improve tests, or reproduce a routing problem.
- **Mobile and accessibility:** test a supported viewport or assistive workflow.
- **Documentation/localisation:** clarify an approved fact without expanding product claims.
- **Road information:** document a public segment with provenance and non-sensitive evidence.
- **Research:** propose a method, dataset, or evaluation with compatible rights.
- **Beta feedback:** report reproducible behaviour from the currently supported area.

## First contribution

1. Read the root `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md`, `SECURITY.md`, and
   `GOVERNANCE.md`.
2. Search existing issues and choose `good first issue` or `help wanted`.
3. Comment with the path you want to take and any accessibility/support need.
4. Wait for scope confirmation before substantial work.
5. Follow the issue acceptance criteria and submit only the relevant change or evidence.
6. Remove credentials, personal location data, production data, faces, and licence plates.
7. Run the checks requested by the issue and state what you did not test.

## Non-technical road contribution

Provide:

- municipality and public road/segment, avoiding a private or home address;
- car, motorcycle, bicycle, or applicable mode;
- observation date;
- condition category and short factual description;
- source, permission/licence, and confidence;
- safely captured, non-sensitive evidence if available.

Your report begins as unverified. It may be corrected or rejected, and it will not change
routing until the moderation process accepts it. Do not collect evidence or use the app
while driving or riding.

## Product language

Use:

- “current Milan beta” for tested coverage;
- “known road-surface information” and “lower estimated exposure”;
- “planned” or “long-term vision” for national coverage, natural-language preferences,
  native apps, CarPlay, Android Auto, live hazards, or a commercial API.

Do not say “safe route”, “prevents accidents”, “AI-powered” without an implemented and
evaluated feature, or imply that unknown data is favourable.

## What happens next

A maintainer checks scope, reproducibility, privacy, licence, and product honesty. Review
times are best effort. Useful contributions may be recognised in release notes or an opt-in
contributor profile. Recognition does not create employment, equity, payment, endorsement,
or a maintainer role.

Security vulnerabilities and credentials do not belong in a public issue. Follow the
private process in `SECURITY.md`.
