# Motia icon concepts — Draft 1

**Status:** provisional, unregistered, subject to founder review and trademark/visual
similarity clearance.

## Concept A — Adaptive ridge

One continuous path rises, changes direction twice, and resolves forward. It forms a soft
abstract M without enclosing a map pin. This is the strongest concept because it remains
legible in monochrome and directly expresses “routes shaped around you.”

Asset: `docs/business-plan/final/assets/motia-mark-draft-1a.svg`

## Concept B — Twin decision

A single line makes two tighter decision turns before continuing. It communicates active
route comparison but is less calm at small sizes.

Asset: `docs/business-plan/final/assets/motia-mark-draft-1b.svg`

## Concept C — Open valley

The route forms a wider M with an open baseline. It is approachable but risks resembling a
generic analytics waveform.

Asset: `docs/business-plan/final/assets/motia-mark-draft-1c.svg`

## First-round decision

Advance Concept A. Remove decorative nodes, use round joins, preserve continuous geometry,
and test it at 16, 24, 32, 48, and 64 pixels. The wordmark should remain typographic rather
than embedded in the icon.
