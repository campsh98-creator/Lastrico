# Motia brand architecture

## Brand system

| Level | Name | Purpose | Line |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Company | Motia | Mobility-intelligence parent brand | Routes shaped around you. |
| Platform | Motia Routing Intelligence | Preferences, condition evidence, ranking, explanations | Descriptive working name |
| Engine | Motia Routing Engine | Future B2B/API product | Long-term working name |
| Product | Lastrico by Motia | First road-surface-aware navigation product | Smoother roads. Smarter routes. |

Motia is the durable company identity. Lastrico is the focused first product, not a synonym
for the company and not limited by “Milano” in its name. Future products may use “by Motia”
when that endorsement builds trust without erasing their specific audience.

## Name and pronunciation

Motia is a coined name. The provisional spoken guide is **MOH-tee-ah** in English and
**MO-ti-a** in Italian. No linguistic origin or pre-existing meaning is claimed. The name,
domains, corporate availability, trademark registrability, and similarity to existing
mobility brands require professional clearance before external launch.

## Positioning

Motia helps people express route trade-offs that conventional controls do not capture and
turns them into transparent, legal, testable routing preferences.

The language hierarchy is:

1. **Current:** “Lastrico compares candidate routes using known road-surface evidence in a
   tested Milan beta.”
2. **Planned:** “Motia is developing a broader routing-intelligence platform.”
3. **Vision:** “Routes may eventually adapt to richer road, vehicle, context, and personal
   preferences.”

Never state that Motia guarantees safety, prevents accidents, has complete Italian coverage,
or already provides promptable/native/automotive navigation.

## Visual direction

The core motif is one continuous adaptive path forming an abstract **M**. It suggests route
choice and movement without relying on map pins, shields, vehicles, municipal landmarks, or
accident imagery.

## Governance

- The parent mark appears on investor and company materials.
- “Lastrico by Motia” appears on product material during the transition.
- Product claims require evidence and release-state labels.
- New sub-brands require a distinct customer problem, not merely a feature.
- A brand owner maintains master assets, accessibility variants, and approval records.
- Trademark, domain, pronunciation, and visual-similarity review precede public launch.
