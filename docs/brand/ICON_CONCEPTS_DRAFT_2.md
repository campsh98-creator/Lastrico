# Motia icon concept — Draft 2 refinement

**Selected direction:** Adaptive ridge  
**Status:** provisional, unregistered, founder-review pending, trademark clearance required

The second round refines the continuous path into a balanced abstract M:

- one uninterrupted stroke;
- rounded caps and joins;
- no decorative dots or live-sensing implication;
- asymmetric forward exit to suggest adaptation rather than a static monogram;
- readable in one colour and on warm-light or dark backgrounds.

Primary asset: `docs/business-plan/final/assets/motia-mark-draft-2.svg`  
Wordmark lock-up: `docs/business-plan/final/assets/motia-wordmark-draft-2.svg`

## Required production review

- visual-similarity and trademark search;
- founder decision on pronunciation and final name;
- optical drawing by a professional identity designer;
- small-size and accessibility testing;
- icon safe areas for Apple, Android, web, and social uses;
- light, dark, monochrome, and print variants;
- confirmation that the mark remains distinct from transport, fintech, and mapping brands.
