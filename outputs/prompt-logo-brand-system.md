# Execution prompt — Lastrico logo concept

## Objective

Create an original, city-independent visual identity for Lastrico: a road-intelligence and
navigation product that helps people compare routes using road conditions, hazards, comfort,
time, and personal preferences.

## Creative direction

- Build a simple, memorable symbol from a route line and a decision or awareness cue.
- Communicate intelligent choice, trustworthy guidance, movement, and community data.
- Use a modern geometric style that remains legible as an app icon.
- Prefer deep petrol green, warm coral, off-white, and dark charcoal.
- Use the wordmark `LASTRICO` only in the wide brand lockup.
- Produce a standalone symbol that can later become the iOS and Android app icon.

## Avoid

- Milan landmarks, city skylines, national flags, cobblestones as the only concept, cars as
  the only mode, accident imagery, warning-sign clutter, gradients that fail at small sizes,
  and visual similarity to Google Maps, Apple Maps, Waze, or other navigation brands.
- Claims such as “safe route” or “accident prevention”.
- Fine details that disappear below 48 px.

## Deliverables

- One primary wide logo concept suitable for the GitHub README.
- One square app-icon concept derived from the same symbol.
- A short English brand rationale and colour palette.
- Project-local PNG assets with versioned filenames; do not overwrite the historical image
  until the new asset is reviewed.

## Validation

- Inspect at full size and at 48 px.
- Verify exact spelling of `LASTRICO`.
- Verify the design remains recognisable in light and dark contexts.
- Verify there are no Milan-specific elements or copied map-brand motifs.

## Release conditions

- The primary logo is visually clean, original, and city-independent.
- The app icon remains readable without text.
- The selected README asset is referenced from English public documentation.
- The concept is explicitly labelled as a first brand direction, not a registered trademark.
