# Junction audit: origin street → Via Friuli (hand-off to Agent 1 / routing forensics)

Written first, as requested. Question: at the first cross street on the right (~60 m after the start of return-trip route 0),
is a car turning right (SW) from the origin street into Via Friuli legal and connected in OSM, in the archived data and in
current OSM? Origin street = "the origin street" (name and its way/node ids withheld; mapping in the git-ignored
`experiments/phase436-surface-intelligence/data/junction-mapping.local.json`, regenerated by the script below).

## Answer

**Yes — legal and connected, archived and current. VERIFIED.** No OSM-side reason explains why the planner or the app
did not use it. The junction is not blocked by any `oneway`, `access`, `motor_vehicle`, `vehicle`, barrier, calming tag or
`type=restriction` relation that I can find. (What the *routing provider's own graph* contains is outside this audit.)

## Facts (all VERIFIED unless labelled)

1. **Position.** Junction node J lies **65.4 m** along route 0 (route 0 has a vertex exactly on J, offset < 0.5 m; route-0
   vertices at 0, 52.8, 54.6, 58.3, 65.4, 75.3 m). So the driver's turn (58–64 m, per shared-context) happened at J: the
   route's own polyline passes *straight through* J on the origin street. Route 0 only turns right 153 m later onto Viale Umbria.
2. **J is a 4-way node of four ways** (archive extract `milan-citywide-s-east`, Overpass 2026-11-04; identical in current OSM):

| role | OSM way | highway | oneway | lanes / other tags | heading at J | version (archive = current) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| origin street, inbound | withheld (ORIGIN-B, 10.8 m) | residential | yes | maxspeed 30, surface concrete:plates | arrives 111.3° (ESE) | 7 (2026-07-29) |
| origin street, outbound | withheld (ORIGIN-A, 9.9 m) | residential | yes | maxspeed 30, surface concrete:plates | leaves 113.2° | 10 (2026-07-29) |
| Via Friuli, inbound | **264363540** (181 m) | residential | yes | lanes 2, surface asphalt, sidewalk separate | arrives 210.6° (SSW) | 6 (2025-11-15) |
| Via Friuli, outbound | **264363537** (28.7 m) | residential | yes | lanes 2, surface asphalt, sidewalk both | leaves 209.1° (SSW) | 6 (2026-07-29) |

3. **Turn.** Origin street arrives at 111.3°, Via Friuli leaves at 209.1°: signed turn **+97.8° = right turn**, onto the *start*
   of way 264363537 (it begins at J), which is `oneway=yes` in its own direction (J → SSW). Entering it from J is exactly the
   permitted direction. Via Friuli is a straight one-way street through J (264363540 → J → 264363537, 210.6° → 209.1°); the
   origin street is a one-way street through J crossing it. No `access`, `motor_vehicle`, `motorcar`, `vehicle`, `barrier`,
   `traffic_calming`, `oneway:conditional`, `access:conditional` on any of the four ways (both archive and current).
4. **Next junction.** 264363537 ends 28.7 m later at a node shared with a one-way side street (name withheld) and the next Via Friuli
   way 264363542; Via Friuli continues SSW (this is the geometry route 1 follows after the reroute).
5. **Node tags.** No tags on J itself (archive and current). Current OSM, tagged nodes within 100 m of J (ids withheld): 4 × `highway=crossing`
   (marked/zebra, 8–32 m), 2 × `highway=stop` (7 m and 29 m, on origin-street/side approaches), 4 × `barrier=kerb` + `kerb=lowered`
   (9–10 m, pedestrian crossings). None is a physical obstacle for cars. **The archive has none of these**: the citywide
   extracts were fetched with `out skel` and then rewritten by `sanitizeOverpassResponse` (`scripts/osm/expand-milan-citywide.mjs:88-110`),
   which copies only id/lat/lon/version/timestamp per node. 0 of the citywide nodes carry any tag, against 28,443 tagged nodes
   in the pilot (OSM-API) extracts. So Lastrico has no barrier / stop / signal / crossing / `railway=tram_crossing` node data in 100% of Milan outside the 2026-10 pilot box.
6. **Turn-restriction relations.** Archive: the 8 citywide extracts contain **0 relations** (query `way["highway"]` + recurse-down,
   `scripts/osm/expand-milan-citywide.mjs:177`); the 11 pilot extracts contain 1,628 relations incl. 220 `type=restriction`, but the
   junction (lon ≈ 9.209) is outside the pilot box (lon 9.169–9.206), so none applies. Current OSM (Overpass, osm_base
   2026-11-14T13:43:36Z, named query `Via Friuli` + all highway ways sharing a node with it + `rel(bw)[type=restriction]`):
   **1 restriction relation** found, **3123413** (`only_straight_on`, from way 164940306 via node 255322402 to a side-street way
   46845808 at the far end of way 264363540, v2 2013-08-11). None of its members is one of the four junction ways or J. **No restriction touches J.**
7. **Changes since the archive.** None for the junction ways: versions, tags and node lists of the 4 ways are identical
   (current vs. archive). J itself is node version 5 in current OSM but the archive records `version:1` — the sanitizer writes
   `version: el.version ?? 1` for skeleton nodes (`expand-milan-citywide.mjs:95`), so **node versions in the citywide archive are fabricated defaults, not OSM history** (VERIFIED: 5 ≠ 1).
8. **Compiled Lastrico data agree with OSM.** `data/surfaces/milan-tiles/920_4544.json` (+`920_4545.json`) hold the segments
   of ways 264363537 / 264363540 and the origin-street ways as: class `residential`, flags 7 (oneway | motor | bicycle),
   `mdBits 21` = car/motorcycle/bicycle direction `forward`, `maBits 7` (car, motorcycle, bicycle allowed), `confirmed_smooth`
   (asphalt for Via Friuli; concrete:plates → concrete for part of the origin street). Segment geometry = raw OSM geometry
   (checked: the compiled endpoints equal the raw node coordinates; full-precision values withheld, they sit 65 m from the origin). **So Lastrico's own surface/access index has
   the connection and the legal direction right** — the app cannot have excluded Via Friuli for access reasons by its own data.
9. **Where Lastrico does *not* judge legality.** `lib/road-intelligence-scoring.ts:49-50`: "Zero is not a legal-route
   certification; turn and conditional restrictions stay with the provider". The scorer only checks one-way/access *along* a returned
   polyline (`restrictionSample`, `:456-490`); it never generates or removes a maneuver. Whether Via Friuli was a candidate at all
   is therefore a provider/candidate-generation question, not a data question (hand-off to `routing-forensics.md`).
