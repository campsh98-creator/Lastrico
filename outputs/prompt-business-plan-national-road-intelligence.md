# Execution prompt — Lastrico national road-intelligence business plan

## Objective

Produce a concise, investor-readable English business plan for evolving Lastrico from the
current Milan road-surface beta into an Italy-wide, multimodal road-intelligence platform.
The future product should compare routes using more than estimated travel time and let people
express route preferences in natural language.

## Required sections

1. Executive summary.
2. Problem and opportunity.
3. Mission, vision, and responsible product principles.
4. Current product and technical evidence.
5. Future product: road hazards, surface quality, lighting, road class, closures, congestion,
   reliability, comfort, accessibility, weather, vehicle suitability, and user preferences.
6. User segments and jobs to be done.
7. Community contribution flow for non-technical users, moderation, provenance, privacy, and
   anti-abuse controls.
8. Competitive positioning and partnership strategy.
9. National data and AI architecture.
10. Production deployment roadmap.
11. Native iOS/Android, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto roadmap.
12. Business model and go-to-market.
13. Team and operating model.
14. Three-year lean, base, and growth financial scenarios.
15. Funding needs, milestones, KPIs, risks, and mitigations.

## Financial requirements

- Use current official or authoritative pricing where available and cite sources with access
  dates.
- Separate one-time product development from recurring cloud, maps, routing, data,
  moderation, support, compliance, and app-store costs.
- State every assumption; do not invent unsupported market precision.
- Show revenue, cost of service, payroll, operating expenses, cash burn, and indicative
  funding need for lean, base, and growth scenarios.
- Treat forecasts as planning scenarios, not promises.

## Technical requirements

- Clearly separate what the current PWA already does from future capabilities.
- Explain why a nationwide dataset must be versioned, tiled, quality-scored, and moderated.
- Keep deterministic route constraints and safety rules outside the language model.
- Use natural language only to translate user intent into explicit, reviewable route
  preferences.
- Never let unknown data be interpreted as a safe or smooth road.
- Describe real-map options, routing engines, traffic/weather integrations, observability,
  privacy, security, offline resilience, and release gates.

## Automotive requirements

- Use current official Apple and Android documentation.
- State that CarPlay and Android Auto access depends on native application work, platform
  categories, entitlements or declarations, review, safety templates, and continuing policy
  compliance.
- Do not promise approval or a launch date controlled by a platform owner.

## Acceptance criteria

- The plan is entirely English and understandable to both investors and engineers.
- It presents Lastrico as city-independent and Italy-first, with possible international
  expansion.
- It covers cars, motorcycles, bicycles, and future compatible mobility modes honestly.
- It does not claim guaranteed safety, accident prevention, or nationwide readiness today.
- Financial tables reconcile and assumptions are traceable.
- External claims have direct citations.

## Release conditions

- Technical, financial, safety, and open-source reviews are complete.
- All figures are labelled by currency, year, VAT treatment where relevant, and scenario.
- Risks and dependencies are as visible as opportunities.
- The document is committed to the repository but is not represented as audited financial
  advice or a binding investment offer.
